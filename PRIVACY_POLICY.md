# Lotto Intel Pro — Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: 2026-09-23**

Lotto Intel Pro ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy.

## Information We Collect

- **Account Information**: Username, email address, and hashed password when you register.
- **Usage Data**: Ticket history, number generation preferences, and app activity.
- **Device Data**: Anonymous diagnostic data to improve app performance.

## How We Use Your Information

- To authenticate your account and provide app functionality.
- To track your lottery tickets and generate performance statistics.
- To improve the app experience.

## Data Sharing

We do **not** sell your personal information. We do not share your data with third parties for advertising purposes.

## Data Security

Your data is transmitted over HTTPS and stored securely. Biometric data (if enabled) is stored only on your device.

## Your Rights

You may request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us.

## Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue at: https://github.com/vanbalagan-vs/lotto-intel-pro-support/issues

## Lottery Disclaimer

This app does not sell lottery tickets and does not guarantee wins. Lottery outcomes are random.
